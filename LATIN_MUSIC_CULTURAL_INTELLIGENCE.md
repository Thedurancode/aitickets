## Key Cultural Intelligence Features

### 1. **Language Mixing**
The AI naturally incorporates Spanish phrases:
- "Baila toda la noche" (Dance all night)
- "La Madre de Todas las Bandas" (The Mother of All Bands)
- "Nunca antes" (Never before)
- "Fiestas Patrias" (Independence celebrations)

### 2. **Event Timing**
Latin events start later (9pm vs 8pm for general concerts)
- Reflects Latin cultural norms around nightlife
- Doors at 8pm, show at 9pm

### 3. **Age Appropriateness**
- Reggaeton: 18+ (nightclub atmosphere)
- Cumbia: ALL AGES (family-friendly)
- Banda/Mariachi: Family-oriented
- Salsa/Bachata: Adult-focused but not restricted

### 4. **Family Dynamics**
- **Banda/Mariachi**: VIP tables for families (palcos)
- **Cumbia**: Multi-generational appeal
- **Reggaeton**: Singles/young adults only

### 5. **Dance Culture**
- **Salsa/Bachata**: Free dance lessons included
- **Cumbia**: "No experience needed" messaging
- **Banda**: Social dancing expected
- **Mariachi**: Formal listening (not dance-focused)

### 6. **Platform Preferences**
**Facebook dominates** (still primary for Latin communities):
- Salsa: Facebook + WhatsApp groups
- Bachata: Instagram (couples) + Facebook
- Reggaeton: TikTok + Instagram (young urban)
- Banda: Facebook + Latin radio + WhatsApp
- Mariachi: Email + Facebook (formal)
- Cumbia: Facebook + WhatsApp + community radio

**WhatsApp is critical** (community group promotions)

**YouTube is essential** (music videos huge in Latin markets)

### 7. **Cultural Events**
- **Banda**: "Fiestas Patrias" (Mexican Independence)
- **Mariachi**: "Noche Mexicana" (Mexican Night)
- Integration with cultural celebrations

### 8. **Pricing Strategy**
- **Accessible**: Cumbia ($20-45), Salsa/Bachata ($25-50)
- **Premium**: Reggaeton ($45-90 club pricing)
- **Tiered**: Banda/Mariachi ($35-150 with VIP tables)
